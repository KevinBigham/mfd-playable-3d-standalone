# GRIDIRON OVERDRIVE — CONTROLS

Three verbs do everything, and what they do depends on what you are holding. Learn TURBO, PASS and
JUMP and you can play.

---

## GAMEPAD (any standard controller)

| Input | Menus | Offence — ball carrier | Offence — QB in the pocket | Defence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Left stick / D-pad | navigate | move | move | move |
| **A** | confirm | lateral backwards | throw to the highlighted receiver | switch defender |
| **B** | back | hurdle | jump pass | dive tackle / contest a throw |
| **X** | — | dive forward | — | dive tackle |
| **Y** | — | spin | — | power tackle |
| **RB / RT** | — | turbo | turbo | turbo |
| **LB** | — | — | touch (lob) pass | — |
| **LT** | — | — | audible | audible |
| **D-pad ◀ ▲ ▶** | navigate | — | throw left / middle / right | — |
| **Start** | pause | pause | pause | pause |
| **Back/Select** | — | — | — | hide your play call |

### Combinations

| | |
|---|---|
| TURBO + A | **bullet pass** (QB) · **stiff arm** (carrier past the line) · **shove** (defence) |
| TURBO + B | **high hurdle** (carrier) · **power tackle** (defence) |
| LB + A | **touch pass** — high, soft, easier to catch, easier to jump |
| UP + TURBO + JUMP, held before a kickoff | **onside kick** |

## KEYBOARD — PLAYER 1

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** | move |
| **Left Shift** | turbo |
| **Space** | pass / snap / switch defender / lateral |
| **J** | jump / tackle / hurdle |
| **K** | dive |
| **L** | spin / power tackle |
| **U I O** | throw to the left / middle / right receiver |
| **N** | touch (lob) pass modifier |
| **Q** | audible |
| **E** | motion a receiver |
| **Tab** | next play page |
| **H** | hide your play call |
| **Esc** | pause |

Every one of these is remappable in **Settings → Controls**.

## KEYBOARD — PLAYER 2 (same keyboard)

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **Arrow keys** | move |
| **Right Shift** | turbo |
| **Numpad 0 / Enter** | pass / snap / switch |
| **Numpad 1 / 2 / 3** | jump / dive / spin |
| **Numpad 4 / 5 / 6** | throw left / middle / right |
| **Numpad 7 / 8 / 9** | lob / audible / motion |

Two players on one keyboard works, but controllers are much better. Any mix is allowed.

## LOCAL MULTIPLAYER (up to four)

Assign seats on the **Quick Play → Players** screen. Each of the four seats is OFF, HOME or AWAY.
Empty seats are played by the CPU. Seat 1 falls back to the keyboard when no controller is plugged
in; seat 2 falls back to the arrows-and-numpad layout.

**Two humans on the same team:**
- On offence, the first seat drives whoever has the ball; the second seat drives a receiver and can
  run him anywhere — he ignores the called route, so he is a genuine free target.
- On defence, each seat drives its own defender and can switch independently. Two seats can never
  end up controlling the same athlete.

**Who am I?** Every human-controlled athlete has a coloured ring at his feet and a numbered badge
above his head — blue P1, red P2, yellow P3, green P4 — and his turbo bar is labelled with his
jersey number at the bottom of the screen.

**Controllers can come and go.** Unplugging a pad mid-play does not stall the match: that athlete
reverts to AI on the next tick and the seat reclaims him when the pad returns.

## PLAY SELECTION

| Input | Effect |
|---|---|
| Move | choose a play in the 3×3 grid |
| A / Space | call it |
| TURBO | next page (three offensive pages, two defensive) |
| JUMP | mirror the play left↔right |
| Back / H | hide your cursor from the person next to you |
| X (4th down) | punt |
| Y (4th down) | field goal |

Both sides pick at the same time behind their own panel. The timer bar across the bottom is 12
seconds; if it runs out, the CPU picks for you.

## AT THE LINE

- **Snap** with PASS/A whenever you are ready — there is no play clock unless you turn one on.
- **Audible** with LT/Q to switch to one of three preset calls.
- **Motion** with L3/E to shift a receiver before the snap.

## KICKING

Field goals and punts use a moving meter. Press PASS/A when the marker is at the top for maximum
accuracy or distance; the stick aims. The meter runs twice and then kicks itself, badly.
